## Defending the Prioritization

* **Legal & Revenue Catastrophe:** A single misrepresentation of SOC2 compliance or Enterprise pricing during procurement or security review can derail a 6-figure enterprise deal or expose the company to breach-of-contract claims.
* **High Operational Frequency:** Affecting 15% of total evaluated queries (3/20), this is an active production risk directly impacting customer-facing interactions, not an isolated edge case.
* **Irreversible Erosion of Trust:** While minor tone errors or entity misclassifications can be remedied with prompt tweaks, misrepresenting security compliance permanently destroys C-suite and buyer confidence.
