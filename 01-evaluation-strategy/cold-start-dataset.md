## Lab1
20 example evaluation rows (10 "good" concise/faithful summaries and 10 "bad" verbose, inaccurate, or poorly formatted summaries), complete with a one-line evaluation reason for each:

| Input Email | Candidate Summary | Label | Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1.** Hi Team, Quick reminder that our quarterly sync has been moved from Thursday 2 PM to Friday at 10 AM EST. Same Zoom link. Please update your calendars. | Quarterly team sync rescheduled to Friday at 10 AM EST (same Zoom link). | **Good** | Accurate, concise, and captures all key details without fluff. |
| **2.** Dear Alex, Thanks for sending over the draft proposal. I've reviewed section 3 and left a few comments on pricing. Could you address those by EOD tomorrow so we can submit to the client? | Alex requested to address pricing comments on section 3 of draft proposal by EOD tomorrow. | **Good** | Faithfully captures the action item, deadline, and context. |
| **3.** Hey Sarah, I won't be able to make it to lunch today because I'm stuck in a customer support call. Let's reschedule for next Tuesday. | Sarah cancelled lunch due to a customer call and proposed meeting next Wednesday. | **Bad** | Inaccurate sender/recipient role and wrong proposed date (Wednesday instead of Tuesday). |
| **4.** Hi All, The office Wi-Fi will be down for scheduled maintenance tonight between 8 PM and 10 PM. Please save your work beforehand. | This email is an announcement to all staff members letting everyone know that the primary office Wireless Fidelity network infrastructure will be undergoing routine scheduled system maintenance this evening starting precisely at 8:00 PM and concluding at 10:00 PM, and employees are advised to save all work. | **Bad** | Extremely verbose and repetitive; restates simple info with unnecessary wordiness. |
| **5.** Hi John, Flight original departure was 4:15 PM, but gate agent said it's delayed to 6:30 PM due to thunderstorm in Chicago. Connection in Dallas might be tight. | Flight delayed from 4:15 PM to 6:30 PM due to Chicago storms; tight Dallas connection expected. | **Good** | Succinctly highlights delay time, cause, and impact. |
| **6.** Hi Security Team, We detected suspicious login attempts on Admin account #402 at 03:14 UTC from IP 192.168.1.50. Account has been locked temporarily. | There were some logins detected on the system. | **Bad** | Too vague; omits critical severity, account ID, and immediate locking action. |

| **10.** Can someone please bring extra whiteboard markers to Room 302 for the workshop? | The sender of this email is writing to inquire if an available team member would be kind enough to bring additional whiteboard markers to conference Room 302. | **Bad** | Contains excessive meta-talk and conversational filler. |
| **11.** Hello Sales, We are looking to buy 50 enterprise licenses for your software. Could you provide a price quote and volume discount details? | Customer requested price quote and volume discount info for 50 enterprise licenses. | **Good** | Clear, concise capture of commercial intent and quantity. |
