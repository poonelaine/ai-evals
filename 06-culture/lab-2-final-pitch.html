Why ship

Three arguments support shipping with conditions.

1. Buyer trust

The P0 failure is a wrong Enterprise price. The launch is built around stopping that one failure first.

2. Business risk

A zero-tolerance Hard gate plus a feature flag on Soft-gated capabilities limits the damage a wrong answer can do.

3. Eval readiness

The pricing control exists, but the CI gates are not yet green. That is why this is conditional, not unconditional.

Source: Ship/Hold memo9
Conditions

Four conditions close the gaps the evidence shows.

A
Clear the CI block

PR #218 stays unmerged until Faithfulness is ≥95% (now 87%) and Tool Selection is ≥95% (now 88%).

B
Prove the pricing gate

100% on the full pricing-tagged golden set, not one case and two controls.

C
Keep Soft-gated features flagged

Entity (≥90%) and trajectory (≥95%) stay behind the feature flag until their gates pass.

D
Close the drift gap

Drift monitoring live by 31 Mar 2027. Owner: Marketing Manager.
